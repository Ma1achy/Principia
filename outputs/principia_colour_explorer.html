Principia — Colour Composition Explorer

Shape-sphere colour-map editor. Build a base map — a SiteBlend (sites × kernel × colour-source × space; every colour map + hard pattern) or a FieldRamp (scalar field × ramp) — then stack overlays over it. The graph compiles to WGSL over a shared library that custom shaders can also call. This is the n̂ → colour editor: one render mode of the eventual catalogue.

Kernel is support × temperature: vmf(κ) blends all sites, topk(k,ks) the nearest k, nearest the argmax (κ→∞). Swatches blend in OKLab (chroma sinks toward site boundaries → uncertainty desaturates); gradients typically blend in RGB. Composition mode's physics-overlay sites are IC-dependent — move the mass slider and the BC/Euler/Lagrange blobs migrate. No "Stability × Hue": BC-distance is shape geometry, not a dynamical metric.
