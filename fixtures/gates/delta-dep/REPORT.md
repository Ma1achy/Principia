## Beside: the relative equilibria and the Kepler-embedded triple (in neither population)

The Lagrange orbits' shapes are fixed points in exact arithmetic: at f64 their chords stay near zero. Equal masses and (0.5, 0.3, 0.2) are linearly unstable (Routh), so at f32 rounding seeds the growth: the equal-mass chord passes `δ_dep` within the horizon and departs, and (0.5, 0.3, 0.2)'s, on the same growth, is below it at the horizon, so whether it departs depends on the horizon. (0.98, 0.01, 0.01) is stable. The Kepler-embedded triple is a real moving orbit, the same chord at f32 and f64: its excursion is below `δ_dep`, so it never departs and its closure registers stay unset.
