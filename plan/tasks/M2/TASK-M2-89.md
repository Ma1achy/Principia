## Deliverables
- The builder in `crates/engine`: a quad's table built from `decode_only_with` on threads, narrowed once.
- Measurement M-A's benchmark and its report: every run's rate, the median and the slowest, per chart, on one thread and on all cores.

## Acceptance tests
- `cargo test -p engine forward_table_builder` — a quad's table built from `decode_only_with` on threads, narrowed once, is bitwise equal to the per-sample evaluation (REQ-DEC-059).
- `cargo xtask bench forward_table` — on the Mac, 1920 × 1080 samples per chart, 5 warm runs, every run's rate reported with their median and the slowest. One thread: PASS if the slowest of the 5 runs reaches 8.1·10⁶ samples a second on every chart of the first slice, never read off the median alone. All cores, the whole-screen criterion: PASS if the slowest of the 5 all-core runs reaches 4.97·10⁸ samples a second on every chart; otherwise SHORT, reported with every run's rate and the factor short per chart; benchmark: waits for the human's Mac (R-186) (REQ-PERF-121).
