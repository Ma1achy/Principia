## Goal
The axes computed from the window, non-visual, in the engine, on the CPU: in a blend a dual ruler, one axis line with the starting chart's real ticks on one side and the target chart's on the other, each fading with its share; on a tilt or rotation within one chart, one number in window units; never a summed number, every true value on hover (R-940). No small term is dropped from a name, and the computed axis is the primary scale on every chart and frame (R-942). It sits beside TASK-M2-81 (path re-timing and alignment); TASK-M8-130 draws it.

## Acceptance tests
- `cargo test -p engine computed_axes` — seven tests, each with a control: (1) ends exact: at a morph's `s = 0` and `1`, selected by REQ-CHART-113's exact comparison, the name and every tick value bitwise the unblended chart's (control: the arithmetic blend at `s = 1`, which differs on some pairs); (2) linear coordinates: the chart's own coordinate, evaluated by the CPU evaluator at each tick's screen position, equals the tick's round value within 4 ulps of `max(1, |v|)` (control: ticks offset by one pixel); (3) warped and per-pixel quantities (`E` on `InvariantLE` under a tilt, R-471 E3): ticks placed by lines § 8b's solve, the residual within REQ-CHART-198's tolerance, and this test waits for that value (control: placement linear in `t`); (4) navigation: a pan moves each linear coordinate's tick values by the pan's component along it, a zoom by 2 halves the spacing, a rotation and a tilt under either R-474 map give the terms of `Q`'s columns; (5) marks: a seam where the value crosses a period, a fold at `L_z = ±L_max`, a clamp run of constant values, a saturated stretch only where REQ-CHART-088's tolerance or R-599's judgement puts it, never a hand-picked point (control: each mark suppressed is detected); (6) exactness on a mixed axis: a contour of the axis's own expression lies on its major ticks (lines § 12; control `h + 0.001`); (7) terms: with no drop, the named shares sum to 1.
