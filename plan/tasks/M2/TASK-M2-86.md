## Goal
The smallest piece that puts a real kernel chart through the system R-926 rules and checks it against today's decode, so the prototype's forward numbers are re-measured on kernel charts: the kernel's latent chart (`crates/kernel/src/chart/latent.rs`), the shape sphere's exponential map (`chart/shape_sphere.rs`) and the `(L_z, E)` chart (`chart/energy.rs`). No new type: today's charts, today's `Window`, today's record layout. No backwards table, no slopes, no blend, no time coordinate, no shader.

## Deliverables
- The slice's harness in `crates/engine`: per sample `decode_only_with` (`crates/engine/src/decode_dispatch.rs`) in f64 at the sample's `(u, v)`; the per-quad forward table the `Ic<f64>` of each sample, one node per sample (RQ-766 (a), R-935); each narrowed once to `Ic<f32>` and written with `write_ic` (`stage/decode.rs`) as the record; integrated with the CPU reference `kernel::driver::wrapper::integrate` at f32 for a fixed horizon `T` and the default occupant.
- The report: every measure below, on the `(L_z, E)` chart split by whether the pixel's neighbourhood crosses one of the chart's kinks (the feasibility edge, the floor and `z_α = 0`), the seed in force named (after TASK-M2-93, `w = r/√I`), and the outcome PASS, EXPLAINED DIVERGENCE or UNEXPLAINED, whichever way it falls.

## Acceptance tests
- `cargo test -p engine first_slice` — predicates, PASS required: (1) the host-written records of a quad equal `write_ic` of the same narrowed `Ic<f32>`, word for word; (2) two runs of the slice give the same records, bitwise; (3) each narrowed component is the correctly rounded value of its f64 evaluation (within half an f32 ulp of it); (4) equal records integrate to equal `SimState`s, bitwise. Compared by bound, measured and reported, not asserted: the narrowed f64 records against today's CPU f32 decode (`sample_with::<…, f32>`) and against the GPU decode stage on Metal (`DecodeStage::run`), per component in f32 ulps at `max(1, |x|)`; the branch selects (pole and upper/lower selects, and on the `(L_z, E)` chart its feasibility and sheet selects) that differ between the two; for differing records, the integrated outcome class and `t_end_step` after `T`; and at each zoom (home, 2¹⁰, 2²⁰, where 2²⁰ is R-90's `ℓ_switch = 20`) the share of neighbouring sample pairs whose narrowed records are bitwise identical. The control arm, measured beside the comparison (R-84): the same narrowed records each moved by one f32 ulp in one component, integrated for the same `T`; the share of outcome-class and `t_end_step` flips it gives is the chaos baseline, and a flip rate between today's f32 decode and the narrowed f64 records is read against this baseline, never alone. Criterion, written before any run: PASS if predicates 1 to 4 hold, no branch select differs between today's f32 decode and the narrowed f64 records, every record where the two differ is listed with its size, and REQ-PERF-120's rate is met on all three charts; EXPLAINED DIVERGENCE if a predicate fails, or a branch select differs, with a named cause (for example the f32 `atan2`'s integer path at a pole select, R-437), or the rate falls short with a named cost; UNEXPLAINED otherwise. The Mac only; no Windows runner job (REQ-DEC-057).
- `cargo xtask bench first_slice` — on the Mac (R-186), 1920 × 1080 samples per chart, one thread, five warm runs, every run's rate reported; the slice's PASS needs the slowest of the five to reach 8.1·10⁶ samples a second on all three charts; a shortfall is reported with its named cost (EXPLAINED DIVERGENCE) or as UNEXPLAINED, and nothing is tuned to pass; benchmark: waits for the human's Mac (R-186) (REQ-PERF-120).
