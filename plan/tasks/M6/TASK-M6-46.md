## Goal
On the mock engine, the View menu gains **Debug view**, a check entry after F3 hide, a `SetField` marked "no history" (R-467 D5). With it off, the presentation view, the region the mock's test hook flags draws the canvas background (the window's clear colour) with no hatch, and the outcome legend holds the nine classes with no "forbidden region" key. With it on, the flagged region draws the hatch and the legend adds a "Debug" key, its three entries with their counts. The hook stays, `MockCanvas::flag_invalid`, and can now be turned on at launch with `PRIN_MOCK_INVALID="s₀,t₀,s₁,t₁"`; a region inside `[0,1]²` logs the console's decoder-bug warning once per view (R-467 D7, D8).
