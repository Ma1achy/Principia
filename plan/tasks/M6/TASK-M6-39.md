# TASK-M6-39 — Live drags both ways: every frame, one undo entry per drag, the compass's mode switched by the slider dragged (R-458); the slice drag follows the pointer (R-465)

- **Pitfalls:** none
- **Size:** ~550 lines

## Goal
On the mock engine, every drag is live and one undo entry (R-458). Today a compass drag is previewed in the compass alone and sent once on release, so the sliders and the figure jump at the end (`compass.rs`'s `drag_stopped`, on `main` and in PR #240), while every slider sends an edit a frame with nothing coalescing them, so a slider drag is one undo entry per frame, against R-96. And the compass's mode switches only when a slider's value changes, so it misses a held thumb, a slider at its end and a number being typed. This task makes the contract's history coalesce a drag into one entry (gui_state_contract §2), sends each drag's edit every frame (at most one a frame), logs a drag once, and switches the compass's mode on a drag's first frame, on the reworked compass (R-451, TASK-M6-33).

It also builds R-465's slice drag (RQ-368's option (b), the human's). The reworked compass moves the slice by the drag's horizontal component, as R-451 B3 has it, so untilted, where the drawn normal points to the screen's lower left, a drag to the right moves the plane left and down, against the pointer. Under R-465 the slice-mode drag is projected onto the drawn normal's screen direction `ŝ`, so the drawn plane moves with the pointer at every tilt, at B3's rate along `ŝ`, the component across it doing nothing; near the view axis, where `ŝ` has no reliable direction, the drag reads its vertical component (R-465 N3). Tilt mode's drag is unchanged, and R-451 B4's easing stays on the cube's drawing alone (R-465 N1).
