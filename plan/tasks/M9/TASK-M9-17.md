## Goal
The replication suite's targets P-1, P-2, P-3, P-4, P-5, P-6 run as engine tests (`principia_dd_replication.md`; R-594, R-629). At displacement δ = 5×10⁻⁸ around the Burrau start, sampled as Aarseth et al. 1994a did (six circles × 100 azimuths per body), the regular ring of β must be complete around m₁ (≈ 70°) and horseshoe-shaped around m₂ and m₃ (Figs. 2 and 3; Table II) (P-1). Aarseth et al. 1994a's Table I, escape counts and mean escape time ⟨T⟩ per ring, must be reproduced as distributions over the same sampling, the counts read as the predictable fraction (P-2). Escape time against azimuth, T(α), on each ring must fall into the paper's four classes, with the regular β regions coinciding with regular T (Fig. 4) (P-3). Tables II–VII per ring (the paper's β, A, E, P, I and R) must be reproduced as distributions; Table VII is their integrator's floor and is recorded, not a target (P-4). Table VIII's escaper shares, 74.6 / 15.5 / 9.9 % over the 831 predictable escapers, must be reproduced as a distribution (P-5). The large oscillations of T at the boundaries of the three regular regions the paper scanned finely (p. 8) must appear in a zoomed polar sector (P-6). Each tolerance is a calibration, proposed here with its evidence and confirmed by the human with the M9 report (R-71).
