## Goal
M9 exits on this report, not a gate (R-650). It lists every M9 target with its tier, its outcome (PASS, EXPLAINED DIVERGENCE or UNEXPLAINED), its like-for-like checks and, for an explained divergence, the measurement showing the mechanism. A target not yet scored, waiting on a ruling or data, is listed "not scored" with what it waits on, never as a failure. Every M9 calibration is put to the human with it, or, where its proposal has not landed (REQ-SYS-095, TASK-M9-39's, or the calibration of a target listed "not scored"), listed with what it waits on, and the owner reviews it.
