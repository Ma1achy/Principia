## Goal
Play preview runs the sequence through the live frame loop from the timeline cursor, link parameters and dissolves as cuts (R-520 F3), under its banner, as a transient view the engine plays from one preview request: no SetField is sent and nothing enters the history, and the user's baseline cover stays pinned so returning is never blank (REQ-GUI-379). The proof strip renders one small frame per keyframe and per segment midpoint through the blocking exporter at its lowest tier, each bitwise the exported frame, as a lower-priority, cancellable background job. Export… opens the planner first, one row per segment with class, frames, cost, flags and an estimate that carries its basis (the cost over quality_device_note §2's throughput model under its §9 provenance, one value with its provenance key, or "no measurement on this machine yet", never a number), then the export's parameters, Start in the background with progress, ETA, thumbnail and Cancel; the `.principia-seq.json` is written beside every export (R-577).
