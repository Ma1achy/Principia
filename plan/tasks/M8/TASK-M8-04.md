## Goal
`crates/gui` exists as the egui dev binding and cannot reach past the engine's state surface: the engine marks only SimConfig / RenderState / ViewUI, `set_field` and the snapshot `pub`; the gui crate reads snapshots and emits SetFields with no in-process shortcut; the engine never depends on the gui crate. F3 toggles the debug menu inside the same binary. CI lints hold the naming rules from the first GUI commit: nothing is named after a camera, and nothing in the legend or overlays is called "fate".
