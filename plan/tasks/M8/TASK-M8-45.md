## Goal
The Chazy stains' legends, generated from the stain like every legend (render GUI spec §G6; R-442; the Chazy
drill-down §10.10): each class with its share of the view, the title carrying the playhead, "Final motion (as of
t = …)", B labelled "bound, or nobody has left yet", and the status legend naming the working states in full.
R-443 adds the one-body views' legends (the drill-down §10.11): the body lens names its body, the escaper map its
bodies, the rank views their ranks, and a rank view that fell back to the label view says so (on an equal-mass chart,
that it is the label view); and the body lens for k = 0, 1, 2 as three panels side by side at one playhead (§10.11.3).
