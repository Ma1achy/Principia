## Goal
The Chazy stains' legends, generated from the stain like every legend (render GUI spec §G6; R-442; the Chazy
drill-down §10.10): each class with its share of the view, the title carrying the playhead, "Final motion (as of
t = …)", B labelled "bound, or nobody has left yet" in every final-motion legend, the status legend naming the working
states in full, and the energy-channel stains carrying §G6's separate brightness bar. R-443 adds the one-body views'
legends (the drill-down §10.11): the body lens names its body as body 1, 2 or 3, the escaper map its bodies as body 1,
2, 3, the rank views their ranks, the bound row "bound, or nobody has left yet", the energy lens its flat role key with
a `q_k` bar, and a rank view that fell back to the label view says so (on an equal-mass chart, that it is the label
view); and the body lens for k = 0, 1, 2 as three panels side by side at one playhead, titled body 1, 2, 3 (§10.11.3).
Every body name a person reads is 1-based, through the one display conversion of the display rule of 16 Oct 2026
(ruled on PR #226).
