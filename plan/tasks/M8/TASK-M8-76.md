## Goal
The click inspector runs the clicked point four ways (R-574): f32, the survey kernel's one-pixel GPU dispatch, bitwise what coloured the pixel; f64 by `computeIC`, under the f32 run's sim key, occupant and step rule (never RK45); and each nudged by one ulp per component of its decoded IC. It reads the shape-sphere chord between them at the sync boundaries for f32–f64, f64–f64′ and f32–f32′, and marks "faithful until" (10⁻³) and "parted at" (0.1), the human's values, shown and editable, with the self-check. It shows the runs (f64 solid, f32 dashed), the separation plot, the marks, the starting gap, the fates side by side, and, when a trajectory is clicked, the trust line about the atlas pixel. The atlas does not change.
