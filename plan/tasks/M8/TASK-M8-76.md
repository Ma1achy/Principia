## Goal
The click inspector runs the clicked point four ways (R-574): all under the clicked trajectory's own sim key, occupant and step rule (never RK45): f32, the survey kernel's one-pixel GPU dispatch, bitwise what coloured the pixel only for an atlas pixel of the current view, whose IC alone is decoded, and from the IC rounded to f32 for any other IC (while the view source is Result no pixel is an atlas pixel, and a result-view pixel runs from its grid cell's IC under the result's SimConfig; a kept orbit is an atlas pixel only if it was kept from an atlas pixel of a live view, the view source is Live now, and its view is bitwise unchanged); f64 by `computeIC`; and each nudged by one ulp per component of its starting IC. It reads the shape-sphere chord between them at the sync boundaries for f32–f64, f64–f64′ and f32–f32′, and marks "faithful until" (10⁻³) and "parted at" (0.1), the human's values, shown and editable, with the self-check, the estimate and the self-check's λ taken from the `ftle` the IC's own f32 dispatch writes, for an atlas pixel bitwise its nominal copy's (none when the FTLE tier is off, `ftle_valid` is false, or a valid `ftle` is ≤ 0). It shows the runs (f64 solid, f32 dashed), the separation plot, the marks, the starting gap, the fates side by side, and, when a trajectory is clicked, the trust line: for an atlas pixel, about the atlas pixel; for any other IC, the "Not an atlas pixel:" forms, which carry no atlas claim. The atlas does not change.
