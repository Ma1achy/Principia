## Goal
The f32 Benettin shadow's displacement form, as R-974 (RQ-793 (a)) rules it: a joint step on the occupant interface, `x` bit-identical to `step(x)` and `δ⃗` advanced through the same stages, the force difference formed from pair separations, plainly in f32 (a1) or cancellation-free (a2) as a pre-registered measurement chooses; the shadow's projection, energy and collision test formed from `x + δ⃗` where they need absolute positions, defined here under physics review; built beside TASK-M3-14's absolute form and tested against it on the known-Lyapunov periodic orbits, switching to displacement only if FTLE accuracy meaningfully improves (REQ-VAL-038). The definition is written into dd_integrator § 3.8 (R-72) as the ruling has it. Nothing named `Displacement` stays that is not one: TASK-M3-14's `Displacement` variant, the `shadow-form` gate's column of that name and `shadow_forms_agree` are replaced or removed once the form is built (physics review 5866988394 of PR #493, N6). Under an occupant that owns its time mapping (Heggie × RK4, the default, `dτ = S^{3/2}/(R₁R₂R₃) dt`, Part 2b, R-161; logH alike) one `Δτ` moves the sample and its shadow by different physical `Δt`, so this task also defines, under physics review (R-72; REQ-VAL-355), how the joint step keeps the two at the same physical time; the options for that definition include carrying `δt` as a component of the displacement, resynchronised at each renormalisation, or stepping the shadow's `τ` so that it lands on the sample's `t`. It also re-runs REQ-INT-076's pre-registered eight-orbit calibration, unchanged, once this form and R-972's (d) + (a) are built, and proposes `min_regions` ≥ 6 (R-976).
