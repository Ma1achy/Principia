# GUI reviewer — checklist

Named on every task that closes a GUI-area requirement or any `GUI screenshot` verify (`plan/WORKFLOW.md` § "Task files"). The
authority order is: rulings in `decisions.md`, then `GUI_DESIGN_NOTES.md`, then the twelve artboards; the notes win over
the pictures and a ruling wins over both (`docs/gui/design/GUI_DESIGN_NOTES.md` § "Dev GUI (egui) — design notes for the artboards"; `docs/gui/principia_render_gui_spec.md` § "Principia — Dev GUI (egui / F3)").

## 1. Rules that hold everywhere

- [ ] egui is a debug layer toggled with F3 over the wgpu render, sharing the engine's `wgpu` context and surface; it has the final UI's structure and every feature. `docs/gui/design/GUI_DESIGN_NOTES.md` § "Rules that hold everywhere"; `docs/gui/principia_render_gui_spec.md` § "G1. Rules that hold everywhere"
- [ ] Every control reads a `Snapshot` and sends a typed `SetField`; data flows one way, UI → `SetField` → core → snapshot → UI, and no control touches simulation internals. `docs/gui/design/GUI_DESIGN_NOTES.md` § "Rules that hold everywhere"; `docs/gui/principia_render_gui_spec.md` § "G1. Rules that hold everywhere"
- [ ] Navigation is chart construction: pan, zoom and slice edit `z₀` and the basis; no control, window, log line or type is named after a camera. `docs/gui/principia_render_gui_spec.md` § "G1. Rules that hold everywhere"; `docs/gui/principia_render_gui_spec.md` § "G13. Where the artboards are overridden"
- [ ] Every preview of the slice keeps the viewport's aspect (square for the slice); none stretches or crops it. `docs/gui/design/GUI_DESIGN_NOTES.md` § "Rules that hold everywhere"; `docs/gui/principia_render_gui_spec.md` § "G1. Rules that hold everywhere"
- [ ] The figure is never covered: no toasts or labels over the plot; warnings go to the footer console. The hover label and lock reticle are the figure's own marks, and the Home button in its top-right corner is the one control over it (R-466 K7). `docs/gui/principia_render_gui_spec.md` § "G1. Rules that hold everywhere"; `docs/gui/design/GUI_DESIGN_NOTES.md` § "Rules that hold everywhere"
- [ ] Dark default egui theme, Ubuntu / Ubuntu Mono, no styling beyond egui's own. `docs/gui/design/GUI_DESIGN_NOTES.md` § "Rules that hold everywhere"
- [ ] No Chazy subtitles and no object-based stain desk; Stain is the plain node-graph editor. `docs/gui/design/GUI_DESIGN_NOTES.md` § "Rules that hold everywhere"
- [ ] Each control knows whether its field is sim key or render key and warns per field from the caching blast-radius table, not from the struct the field lives in. `docs/gui/principia_render_gui_spec.md` § "G1. Rules that hold everywhere"; `docs/contracts/principia_gui_state_contract.md` § "2. The editable state is the entire coupling surface"; `docs/contracts/principia_caching_contract.md` § "Part 2 — What invalidates what (the dependency graph)"
- [ ] Explore and Stain are the two modes; everything else is a window or a tool; Run settings live in the Run window, not on the page. `docs/gui/principia_render_gui_spec.md` § "G14. Settled by the notes (record)"
- [ ] The outcome legend is called "Legend", never "Fate". `docs/gui/principia_render_gui_spec.md` § "G6. Legend — generated by evaluating the stain (`06_legend.png`)"; `docs/gui/principia_render_gui_spec.md` § "G13. Where the artboards are overridden"

## 2. The state contract: firewall

- [ ] The gui crate depends only on the engine's typed state surface (`SimConfig` / `RenderState` / `ViewUI`, `SetField`, the snapshot); scheduler, cache, kernel and payload buffers are not reachable from it. `docs/contracts/principia_gui_state_contract.md` § "1. The one-way dependency rule"; `docs/contracts/principia_canonical_spec.md` § "1. The substrate (the defining decision)"
- [ ] The engine never imports the GUI; the dependency arrow is GUI → state → engine. `docs/contracts/principia_gui_state_contract.md` § "1. The one-way dependency rule"
- [ ] egui holds no `&mut` into engine state even though it is in-process; it earns no shortcut the TS binding would not have. `docs/contracts/principia_gui_state_contract.md` § "1. The one-way dependency rule"
- [ ] The boundary is data, not objects: no live handle to an engine struct is kept by the GUI, only the last snapshot. `docs/contracts/principia_gui_state_contract.md` § "1. The one-way dependency rule"; `docs/design/principia_systems_architecture.md` § "3. The membrane — the deployment view (demoted, not diminished)"
- [ ] The snapshot is GUI-sized (view state, tier, a few scalars), throttled to ~10 Hz; egui redraws at frame rate from the latest one; no payload, tree or reduction array is in it. `decisions.md` § "R-94 — The GUI snapshot is ~10 Hz *(closes RQ-45)*"; `docs/contracts/principia_gui_state_contract.md` § "1. The one-way dependency rule"
- [ ] No GUI element holds sim logic, caches sim data or computes anything the engine needs. `docs/contracts/principia_gui_state_contract.md` § "1. The one-way dependency rule"
- [ ] The engine could be driven from a JSON of `SimConfig + RenderState` with no GUI attached (the PR's GUI feature has a non-GUI path to the same edit). `docs/contracts/principia_gui_state_contract.md` § "1. The one-way dependency rule"
- [ ] The precision warning and console read what the snapshot and telemetry stream carry (`DECODE_SWITCHOVER` on visible quads, `AT_F32_FLOOR`), not fixed depths. `decisions.md` § "R-54 — The precision warning is event-driven *(GU-3 (b))*"; `docs/contracts/principia_gui_state_contract.md` § "2. The editable state is the entire coupling surface"
- [ ] Tier-3 overlays upload only per-visible-quad metadata (~16 KB design budget) while on; nothing per-sample crosses; finer detail goes to inspector drill-down. `docs/gui/principia_render_gui_spec.md` § "12.1 Structural overlays and tile debug shaders"

## 3. The state contract: where state lives

- [ ] Each new field is placed as the contract says: chart, `z₀ q₁ q₂`, slice values, lock flag, `z_locked`, excursion `δ`, link ids, integrator occupant, `T`/`dt`/thresholds/`eps`, `r_coll`, quality → `SimConfig`; stain graph, overlays, palette/compaction, playhead `t` → `RenderState`; panels, focus, selection, kept orbits, `t_cursor`, debug visibility, transport, linked views → `ViewUI`. `docs/contracts/principia_gui_state_contract.md` § "2. The editable state is the entire coupling surface"
- [ ] Lock is chart construction and lives in `SimConfig`. `decisions.md` § "R-69 — What is undoable *(closes the step-6 open question)*"; `docs/gui/principia_render_gui_spec.md` § "G4. Lock — the reticle and the pin (`08_lock.png`)"
- [ ] Transport (play / pause / speed / loop) is `ViewUI`: not undoable, not on the sim key. `decisions.md` § "R-96 — Colour and GUI definitions *(closes RQ-52 and RQ-54, definitional parts)*"; `decisions.md` § "R-101 — Transport lives in `ViewUI`; the clock writes the playhead without history *(closes RQ-61)*"
- [ ] The link ids in `SimConfig` are the chart's link functions; linked side-by-side views are a separate `ViewUI` item. `decisions.md` § "R-106 — The link ids are the chart's link functions *(closes RQ-66)*"
- [ ] The engine reads no `ViewUI` field. `docs/contracts/principia_gui_state_contract.md` § "2. The editable state is the entire coupling surface"; `docs/contracts/principia_gui_state_contract.md` § "7. What a replacement GUI must honour (the teardown contract)"
- [ ] Quality is the preset selector (Auto / named tiers / Custom) editing `SimConfig.quality`; Custom is pre-filled with the current preset and read-only until touched; the arbiter stays engine-side. `docs/contracts/principia_gui_state_contract.md` § "6. Quality settings — preset selector over one struct (see `principia_quality_device_note.md`)"
- [ ] The fragment registry is the scanned `frag/` directories (a file appearing registers it); `debug/` is a category tag filtered by `ViewUI`, not a separate mechanism; compute occupants are a choice among compiled variants. `docs/contracts/principia_gui_state_contract.md` § "3. The registry is the scanned filesystem — for the *fragment* side; compute occupants are Rust build variants"
- [ ] Slots are typed only by output signature (`colour` → `vec3`, `brightness` → `f32`); no rule forces "vector → colour, scalar → brightness". `docs/contracts/principia_gui_state_contract.md` § "4. The occupant model — typed by signature, free inside"
- [ ] A categorical value is never averaged; mixed pixels resolve colour-per-sample then SSAA. `docs/contracts/principia_gui_state_contract.md` § "4. The occupant model — typed by signature, free inside"

The editable state is the entire coupling surface"

- [ ] `ViewUI`-only state (open panels, focus, selection, kept orbits, transport) never enters the history. `decisions.md` § "R-69 — What is undoable *(closes the step-6 open question)*"; `decisions.md` § "R-96 — Colour and GUI definitions *(closes RQ-52 and RQ-54, definitional parts)*"
