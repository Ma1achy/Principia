# QA reviewer — checklist

Named on every task (`plan/WORKFLOW.md` § "Task files": `code` and `qa` always). The question this reviewer answers: **are the tests
written from the requirements, not from the code?** The docs are the authority; where a requirement and its source
disagree, the source wins and the requirement is the bug (`plan/MILESTONES.md` § "Assumptions").

## 1. Each closed requirement has its test

- [ ] Every requirement id in the task's **Closes** line appears in at least one acceptance line, with its verify method's threshold and fixture, and the PR description gives that command's output. `plan/WORKFLOW.md` § "The unit: one task, one branch, one PR"; `plan/WORKFLOW.md` § "Task files"
- [ ] Each test is derived from the requirement's `statement` and `verify.detail` (read against its cited source sections), not from what the implementation happens to do: the assertion would still be right if the code were rewritten. `plan/MILESTONES.md` § "Assumptions"; `docs/read_first/principia_00_philosophy.md` § "4.5 Verify against the formulation, not just the data"
- [ ] The test runs under the runner its `verify.method` names: `unit test` / `property test` → `cargo test -p <crate>` (property tests with proptest); `golden image` → `cargo xtask golden`, rendered with native wgpu offscreen from M1, with the M8 Playwright browser suite checking against the same baselines within tolerance; `numerical gate` → `cargo xtask gate`; `benchmark` → `cargo xtask bench`; `GUI screenshot` → `cargo xtask screenshot` (on GUI PRs and at the gates); `review checklist` → the named reviewer's checklist or a CI lint that installs the check. `plan/MILESTONES.md` § "M0 — Gates and scaffolding"; `decisions.md` § "R-110 — What CI runs, where, and against which goldens *(closes RQ-79)*"; `plan/WORKFLOW.md` § "Conventions: crates and runners (proposed, RQ-76)"; `plan/WORKFLOW.md` § "Task files"
- [ ] A property test's property is the one the requirement states (e.g. idempotence, constraint preservation, round trip over the full value range), fuzzed over the stated domain including its edges and saturation. `docs/design/principia_dd_generation_root.md` § "5. Tests (properties any generator must satisfy)"; `docs/design/principia_chart_reference.md` § "5.2 Tests that can fail"
- [ ] Where the corpus lists tests for a component (a drill-down's "Unit tests" / "Tests" section), the task's tests cover the ones its requirements cite, by the corpus's own assertions. `docs/contracts/principia_parity_contract.md` § "8. Test index (drill-down suite → tier)"; `docs/design/principia_debug_tooling_plan.md` § "Principle"
- [ ] Each seam the task touches has at least one integration test for its seam-catalogue row. `docs/design/principia_systems_architecture.md` § "5. The seam catalogue"; `docs/design/principia_systems_architecture.md` § "8. What drills down from here"
- [ ] Payload-shaped features are tested on both surfaces — an accessor assertion and a rendered field view — and against synthetic, hand-filled buffers with known values before real components feed them. `docs/design/principia_debug_tooling_plan.md` § "Principle"

## 2. Thresholds and tolerances

- [ ] Every threshold, tolerance and fixture size comes from the requirement, its source section, or its calibration requirement's id ("tolerance: REQ-… (calibrated)") — none is picked in the test. `decisions.md` § "R-71 — A missing value becomes a calibration requirement *(closes RQ-46 to RQ-55, values)*"; `docs/read_first/principia_00_philosophy.md` § "4.2 Every constant must be derived or absent"
- [ ] No threshold was tuned to make the test pass: the PR history shows the threshold unchanged from the first run, or the change has an RQ and a ruling. `docs/read_first/principia_00_philosophy.md` § "4.2 Every constant must be derived or absent"; `plan/WORKFLOW.md` § "Escalation"
- [ ] A threshold on a quantity spanning decades is relative, from the observed distribution or a measured gap, never an absolute cutoff by eye. `docs/read_first/principia_01_pitfalls.md` § "3. Standing rules earned in this sequence"
- [ ] Parity Tier-N tolerances are measured (native in-process `wgpu` spread), not guessed; the §4 figures (~1e-5 decode, ~1e-5 one step, ~1e-6 invariants) are starting points, replaced by measured values. `decisions.md` § "R-85 — Native wgpu sets the Tier-N tolerances *(closes RQ-36)*"; `docs/contracts/principia_parity_contract.md` § "4. Tolerance — and the cross-backend reality"
- [ ] Encode and round-trip tolerances are asserted in physical units, never as a z-residual near saturation. `docs/contracts/principia_inverse_encode_contract.md` § "Part 4 — Conditioning: assert in physical units, never in z"
- [ ] The aggregate CPU-vs-GPU survey test runs on a uniform grid (not quadtree leaves) with a sampling-noise-bounded threshold. `docs/contracts/principia_parity_contract.md` § "5. The aggregate-survey agreement test (what certifies "the picture is the same")"

## 3. Tests that can fail

- [ ] Every new test is shown able to fail: a control (deliberately sign-flipped variant, known-answer field, a comparison that must differ) is run and goes red. `docs/read_first/principia_00_philosophy.md` § "4.4 A test that cannot fail is not a test"
- [ ] No check's reachable output set excludes the failure it guards against: no mask over the bits a fork lands in, no fold seeded at a value equal to the failure, no statistic identical for empty and full. `docs/read_first/principia_01_pitfalls.md` § "9. A PARITY CHECK THAT MASKS THE BITS THE FORK LANDS IN"
- [ ] The measurement can fire before its result is read (e.g. a distinct-value count is not bounded below the claimed effect by construction). `docs/read_first/principia_01_pitfalls.md` § "3. Standing rules earned in this sequence"
- [ ] Where a requirement says "must not", "never" or "refuse", there is a negative test that attempts the forbidden thing and asserts it is refused or absent (e.g. the metadata gate fails generation with the field named; `forbids_energy_normalisation` refuses the config). `docs/design/principia_dd_generation_root.md` § "5. Tests (properties any generator must satisfy)"; `docs/design/principia_chart_reference.md` § "5.2 Tests that can fail"
- [ ] Failure paths are executed, not only written: an OOM / pressure path is run under an artificially low cap; a device-loss or fallback path is triggered. `docs/design/principia_dd_telemetry_and_tiers.md` § "And test it deliberately"
- [ ] A test holding a variable fixed states that domain and is not used as evidence for the regime where it moves (stateless determinism ≠ trajectory labels). `docs/read_first/principia_01_pitfalls.md` § "10. GENERALISING A STATELESS RESULT TO A TRAJECTORY"

## 4. Parity and determinism tests

- [ ] Per-step and per-decode comparisons feed both pipelines the same input (including synthetic states: near-collision, high-substep, near-feasibility-edge) and compare immediately — never accumulate before comparing. `docs/contracts/principia_parity_contract.md` § "3. The load-bearing discipline: never accumulate before comparing"
- [ ] Each parity test is labelled with its tier (L / B / N / S) and asserts only what that tier allows. `docs/contracts/principia_parity_contract.md` § "8. Test index (drill-down suite → tier)"; `docs/contracts/principia_parity_contract.md` § "2. The three tiers"
- [ ] No test asserts pointwise CPU/GPU agreement at `t_end` on a chaotic trajectory; outcome class is asserted exactly only on non-chaotic fixtures. `docs/contracts/principia_parity_contract.md` § "Tier S — structural only (asserting the pointwise gap would assert a falsehood)"; `decisions.md` § "R-84 — Branch decisions across precisions *(closes RQ-35)*"
- [ ] Tier-L "same logic" is verified by inspection of the one source plus the stateless no-fork test, never by agreement of results; no single backend is taken to certify another. `docs/contracts/principia_parity_contract.md` § "Tier L — exact, no tolerance (the bulk of the suite)"
- [ ] Tier-B integer fields are asserted bit-exact only given identical branch decisions and integer inputs. `docs/contracts/principia_parity_contract.md` § "Tier B — integer-exact *given the same branch decisions* (integer & packed fields)"
- [ ] The cross-check against f32 runs only for `t < t_max(f32)` and reports "not applicable" beyond. `decisions.md` § "R-93 — The f32 predictability horizon gates the cross-check only *(closes RQ-44)*"; `docs/design/principia_dd_predictability_horizon.md` § "4.1 The two kernels have different horizons"
- [ ] The firewall test schedules the same region two different ways and asserts byte-identical payloads. `docs/design/principia_systems_architecture.md` § "8. What drills down from here"; `docs/contracts/principia_scheduler_contract.md` § "Part 1 — The firewall: the scheduler is arbitrary about *what* it looks at, never about *what* it sees"
- [ ] Codegen tests run pack∘unpack in Rust and in a GPU self-test dispatch of the WGSL unpack; a hand-edit of a generated file is detected by the generated-file guard. `docs/design/principia_dd_generation_root.md` § "5. Tests (properties any generator must satisfy)"; `docs/design/principia_dd_generation_root.md` § "4. Seams (obligations → integration tests)"

## 5. Physics validation tests

- [ ] Ground truth comes from outside the system (analytic Lagrange/Euler, published periodic orbits, Burrau/Lehto), run on the f64 CPU reference; conservation alone is not accepted as correctness. `docs/notes/principia_validation_ground_truth_note.md` § "Self-consistency ≠ correctness (why this is a separate suite)"; `docs/notes/principia_validation_ground_truth_note.md` § "Four tiers of ground truth (increasing in what they prove)"
- [ ] Non-chaotic cases are checked precisely; chaotic benchmarks are checked at structural features (escaper identity, encounter sequence, approximate ejection time), never pointwise. `docs/notes/principia_validation_ground_truth_note.md` § "Chaos forbids pointwise comparison (exact vs structural)"
- [ ] Known physical ICs enter through canonicalise → encode → round-trip check → forward pipeline (Path A), or by direct physical inject for exactly-degenerate configs (Path B), and the test says which. `docs/notes/principia_validation_ground_truth_note.md` § "Two entry paths (routed by interior-vs-degenerate)"
- [ ] A benchmark comparison records and fixes the `r_coll` the reference assumed. `docs/notes/principia_validation_ground_truth_note.md` § "Open sub-questions (settle at implementation)"; `docs/contracts/principia_integrator_contract.md` § "Part 7 — Detectors as the `SimState` producer"
- [ ] A comparison between occupants holds the stepper, step control and colour-ramp constants fixed across arms and prints full provenance per arm. `docs/contracts/principia_integrator_contract.md` § "The comparison discipline is part of the seam, NOT a convention"
- [ ] Refinement or criterion work is scored in payload space, not in a colour space. `docs/design/principia_dd_refinement_policy.md` § "4. THE METRIC MUST BE PAYLOAD-SPACE. THIS IS NOT OPTIONAL."; `docs/read_first/principia_01_pitfalls.md` § "5. MEASURING IN THE WRONG SPACE — `error(B)` in OKLab"
- [ ] An aggregate quantity quoted as a result is shown to converge under sampling refinement, against the calibrated convergence threshold. `docs/read_first/principia_01_pitfalls.md` § "3. Standing rules earned in this sequence"; `docs/read_first/principia_00_philosophy.md` § "4.5a A quantity that does not converge under refinement is measuring the sampling"
- [ ] A diagnostic re-running trajectories reuses the original discretisation. `docs/read_first/principia_01_pitfalls.md` § "3. Standing rules earned in this sequence"
- [ ] Reports include negative and messy results, scatter and the number of regions sampled. `docs/read_first/principia_00_philosophy.md` § "4.6 Report the negative, and the messy"

## 6. Fixtures, golden images and reference values

- [ ] Every fixture a test uses is named in the task's acceptance line and checked in where the runner reads it (`fixtures/golden/` for golden images, `fixtures/gates/` for gates). `plan/WORKFLOW.md` § "Conventions: crates and runners (proposed, RQ-76)"; `plan/WORKFLOW.md` § "Task files"
- [ ] Golden images are pinned to colour_composition §7.1's list; a preset counts as done only when its golden image matches its render in the reference HTML files (`docs/gui/reference/principia_colour_explorer.html`, `principia_colour_presets.html`) to tolerance. `decisions.md` § "R-122 — The reference HTML files are the colour oracle *(closes RQ-90 and RQ-101)*"; `decisions.md` § "R-16 — The colour PDF's map lists are ported *(closes RQ-14)*"; `docs/design/principia_colour_composition.md` § "7. Preset table & golden-image obligation"; `docs/design/principia_colour_composition.md` § "7.1 The complete map list (R-16)"
- [ ] Golden reference images come from the oracle (the two reference HTML files, `decisions.md` § "R-122 — The reference HTML files are the colour oracle *(closes RQ-90 and RQ-101)*"), never from the implementation under test; LUT data comes from the published matplotlib tables and Moreland's cool-warm table, the Principia stops from the colour explorer.
- [ ] No golden baseline file changes without a recorded gate decision; the M8 browser suite diffs against the native baselines, it does not re-baseline them. `decisions.md` § "R-110 — What CI runs, where, and against which goldens *(closes RQ-79)*"
- [ ] Invalid samples render REQ-COL-055's hatched pattern, which collides with no palette entry. `decisions.md` § "R-132 — The R-71/R-72 classification is accepted, with three changes *(closes RQ-110)*" `docs/design/principia_colour_composition.md` § "7. Preset table & golden-image obligation"
- [ ] Colour-vision simulation golden values come from a published reference implementation, named with its version. `decisions.md` § "R-78 — Real Viénot and Brettel colour-vision simulation *(closes RQ-29)*"; `docs/design/principia_colour_composition.md` § "4.3 Display stage (terminal, outside the pipeline)"
- [ ] Transcribed constants are checked against their primary source before entering the shared source (OKLab against Ottosson; Yoshida-6 against Yoshida 1990). `decisions.md` § "R-51 — The OKLab coefficients are checked against Ottosson *(CO-2)*"; `decisions.md` § "R-57 — The Yoshida-6 coefficients are checked against Yoshida (1990), and the `w₂ < 0` label fixed *(TO-1)*"
- [ ] Shape-map tests check the landmarks numerically and cross-check `n` against the IC Inspector's JS on random ICs. `decisions.md` § "R-14 — One shape-sphere convention, the IC Inspector's *(closes RQ-12, corrects R-12's premise)*"
- [ ] Debug-preset golden tests show literal stored values for debug fields (a failed-state `0.0` reads as `0.0`), with NaN to the invalid colour. `decisions.md` § "R-79 — NaN and sentinels *(closes RQ-30)*"; `docs/design/principia_colour_composition.md` § "6. Debug views as presets"
- [ ] The UV coordinate view and a picking cross-check (click a known corner, assert the expected UV/IC) are tested wherever the task touches a coordinate path. `docs/design/principia_coordinate_conventions_note.md` § "The catch: a UV-passthrough debug view (debug-first)"

## 7. Pitfall regressions

- [ ] Every requirement the task closes whose source cites `principia_01_pitfalls.md` has a regression test (or, for a review-checklist requirement, the physics reviewer's item) that would have caught the original failure. `docs/read_first/principia_01_pitfalls.md` § "Pitfalls — failures that looked like results"; `docs/read_first/principia_00_philosophy.md` § "4.4 A test that cannot fail is not a test"
- [ ] The pitfall ids the task lists in **Pitfalls** each map to at least one test or checklist item in the PR. `docs/read_first/principia_01_pitfalls.md` § "Pitfalls — failures that looked like results"
- [ ] A new failure mechanism found while building is recorded in `principia_01_pitfalls.md` in the same PR. `docs/read_first/principia_01_pitfalls.md` § "4.5 THIS WAS ALREADY ON RECORD"
- [ ] When a fix removes one symptom, the other co-located symptoms are re-measured and reported separately. `docs/read_first/principia_01_pitfalls.md` § "8. TWO ARTEFACTS ARE NOT ONE DEFECT"

## 8. Acceptance and process

- [ ] Every acceptance command in the task file was run on the PR head and passes; CI (build, every runner, `cargo xtask plan-check`) is green. `plan/WORKFLOW.md` § "The unit: one task, one branch, one PR"
- [ ] Nothing is deferred: no acceptance test, requirement, finding or part of the task is skipped, `#[ignore]`d, marked TODO or moved to a later task without a `decisions.md` entry made by the human. `plan/WORKFLOW.md` § "No deferral"
- [ ] No reviewer finding was waived by the implementer or another reviewer. `plan/WORKFLOW.md` § "No deferral"
- [ ] Where the task needed a value, name or behaviour the corpus does not give, there is a REVIEW_QUEUE entry and the requirement carries `rq:`; the test does not fill the gap. `plan/WORKFLOW.md` § "Escalation"
- [ ] A calibration requirement's acceptance includes the human's confirmation at the milestone gate, and the PR marks the value as pending until then. `decisions.md` § "R-71 — A missing value becomes a calibration requirement *(closes RQ-46 to RQ-55, values)*"; `plan/WORKFLOW.md` § "Human checkpoints: the milestone gates"
- [ ] Every earlier milestone gate is still green on this PR. `plan/MILESTONES.md` § "Assumptions"
