Chart position is coloured by a base, then layers on top — on any chart

Chart position is one node and works on every chart, the shape sphere included. Shape n̂ is a different node and keeps its own colouring; the brightness slot, Replace-L and the post chain are as they are. All renders below are the prototype on Latent z_α × z_β, the systems' positions at t = 0, so each shows the colouring itself.
