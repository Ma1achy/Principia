## Notes

- **`bhunt` is not the hunt.** Its atlas label is "New Broucke-region orbits", source `bh2026`, a research note the site
  published on 8 September 2026. It is part of the atlas and is used like any other family.
- **Li, Jing & Liao 2018's title is quoted three ways**: "Over a thousand new periodic orbits of a planar three-body
  system with unequal masses" (this site), "The 1223 new periodic orbits of planar three-body problem with unequal mass
  and zero angular momentum" (Reusser's compilation; arXiv:1709.04775) and "Periodic orbits for Newtonian planar
  three-body problem with unequal mass" (the authors' own page). The DOI, 10.1093/pasj/psy057, is the same in all three.
  Check the title against the journal before it goes in any reference list.
- **"The hunt"** (the site's volunteer search) is not in this snapshot and is not used until the operator publishes it.
- **Source papers come first.** Where a figure or comparison rests on a published orbit, cite the paper, and the atlas
  as where the data came from. A family whose source is the atlas's own note (`bh2026*`, `bhh2026`, `pancake2026`) has
  no other source.
- The Belgrade gallery (three-body.ipb.ac.rs) has its own terms and credit line, in
  `docs/incoming/2026-10-26_belgrade_gallery/README.md`.
