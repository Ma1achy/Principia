# Every figure in Valtonen et al. 2016

One row per figure, all 78. Chapters 3, 4, 7 and 8 are described in detail; the others in one line. Page = book page (PDF page in brackets). **Committed** means the image is in `figures/` because its caption credits Wikimedia Commons or Wikipedia, which the preface (p. vi) says are reproduced under the GNU Free Documentation License / GNU GPL v3 with credit and source required and changes to be indicated. Every other figure is **not** in the repository; a local PNG crop of each was handed over privately (`book_figures/`, index in its `index.csv`).

Credit lines are copied from the captions. The licence of each committed image is the book's statement; the original file on Wikimedia Commons may carry a more specific licence or be public domain, which we have not checked.

Digitisation method, calibration and uncertainty for each digitised figure are in the CSV headers and `scripts/digitise.py`.
