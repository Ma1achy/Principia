From me, Malachy. A plain summary of how the mirror and the shape sphere should work, to sit beside the shape sphere
instruction I sent. If anything in that instruction or my earlier rulings disagrees with this, this is what I mean;
queue the disagreement rather than guessing.

Starting conditions. A triangle and its mirror image play out the same way and end the same way. When you are
building or picking a starting condition, it is fine to know they are twins. In the constructor you can reach both;
the twin is shown as a faint marker so the symmetry is obvious. Nothing is blocked off.

The shape sphere. It is the whole sphere, always. The top half is the triangle one way round, the bottom half is it
flipped, and the equator is where the bodies are in a line. Every point is its own real system. No hiding half of it.

Traces and sound. A moving system crosses the equator whenever the bodies line up, so its trace carries on into the
other half and you see it. Listen already works this way.

The other charts. They were never the problem. Where they cannot show something, they say so and the trace leaves the
chart. Nothing is folded or faked. Do not change them for this.

The rule underneath it. The mirror is a fact about fates ("these two end the same way"). It is not a reason to hide
part of the space a system moves through.

One tidy-up: this supersedes the two mirror clauses of my RQ-498 ruling in rulings_2026-10-21.md ("the t = 0 identity
on the sphere is stated modulo the mirror, under R-141" and "in-square mirrors match to rounding"). On the shape
sphere the identity now holds point for point.
