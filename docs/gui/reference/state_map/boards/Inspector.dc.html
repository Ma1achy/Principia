The colour slot's inspector: the Shape n̂ node, or Chart position's base and layers

The inspector follows the source. From the Shape n̂ node: its panel exactly as today. From Chart position, on any chart including the shape sphere: one base and a stack of layers, offering what exists on that chart (on the sphere, its site sets, lat × lon or Fibonacci cells, lat / lon lines and cell edges). Swap the source and each base takes its counterpart's place (Position ↔ Gradient, Site blend ↔ Site blend, Bands ↔ Palette); every setting stays, so switching back restores it.
