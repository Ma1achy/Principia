Changing the source chart re-types the wires
Switch the source from Chart position to the Shape n̂ node, or Chart position's chart from Latent to Shape sphere and the wire becomes point⟨sphere⟩. The flat base takes its sphere counterpart's place (Gradient → Position, Site blend → Site blend, Palette → Bands); the layers have no sphere form and are kept, ghosted, until the chart is flat again. Switching back restores everything exactly. Picture carries across.
