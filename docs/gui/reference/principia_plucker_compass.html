Each filled cell answers one question: how aligned is my view with the plane spanned by two chart axes? Teal = +, terracotta = −, blank = orthogonal. An axis-aligned view lights exactly one cell at ±1.00. A single tilt slides that weight along a row or column (the cells sharing the untilted index) — no dead spots, exact at every configuration. It shows the plane, not your basis: spinning q₁,q₂ within the view leaves every cell unchanged. It does not show position (z₀) — a compass gives heading, not location.
