Slice mode. The plane is your view — Width (chart x) × Height (chart y) — sliding along the slider axis (Depth). The cube bounds stand in for the slider's min/max.

Tilt mode. Two tilt directions become two axes: Width = q₁'s target, Depth = q₂'s target. The plane is the actual slice cut through the cube at that orientation — it deforms into rectangles and hexagons as you tilt (±90°, sign matters). The third axis is left unnamed on purpose: there's no single coordinate perpendicular to both targets in 8D, so it's just the direction the slice tips through.
