Smart ink

The same five energy contours, three ways. Real outcome map, real contours.
rulePick the ink with the higher WCAG contrast against the local background. The two tie at relative luminance 0.179.
radius · 6 pxThe background is averaged around the line, so one stray pixel never flips the ink.
hysteresis · 0.25The ink switches only once the contrast clearly favours the other side, so it does not flicker on a mottled edge or while panning.
modesLight or dark (shown); or keep the hue and move only its lightness. Labels use the same rule.
